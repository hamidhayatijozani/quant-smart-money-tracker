# Funding Plan — Quant Smart Money Tracker

## Goal
Keep the project running without pretending the current prototype has a live MQL5 feed or proven trading edge.

## Immediate funding path: paid design-partner evaluations
Offer a tightly scoped, paid evaluation to 3 independent traders, trading communities, or small trading-tool teams.

- Initial price: USD 99 per 14-day evaluation.
- Included: walkthrough, demo-feed signal ranking, COPY/WATCH/AVOID explanations, shadow-mode journal review, and a written gap report.
- Explicit exclusions: live MQL5/Myfxbook data unless separately implemented and verified; no broker login; no real orders; no profit guarantees.
- Payment condition: invoice/payment method agreed with the customer and lawful/available to both parties. Do not claim a payment channel is configured until tested.
- Acceptance: customer receives the walkthrough, the agreed evaluation report, and a list of verified versus missing capabilities.

Three evaluations at $99 would gross $297. This is a target, not secured revenue. It is enough to cover a minimal $7.25/month Render worker + 1 GB disk for roughly 40 months before taxes, payment fees, other services, and changes in pricing; do not treat that calculation as a forecast.

## Outreach sequence
1. Contact 20 qualified prospects individually; target people who already monitor multiple signal providers or need trade-signal auditability.
2. Ask for a 15-minute problem interview, not an immediate purchase.
3. Offer a paid evaluation only when the demo addresses a real workflow.
4. Record prospect, problem, next step, objections, and whether money was actually received.
5. Stop or revise the offer if 20 qualified contacts produce no serious conversations.

## Secondary channels
- GitHub Sponsors: configure only if the maintainer's country, identity, and payout method are eligible. Start with $5, $15, and $30 monthly tiers. Sponsorship is not guaranteed income.
- Render for Startups: the official Prototype offer advertises $500 credits but requires a new customer, less than four years in operation, less than $50M raised, and at least $25K in seed funding. Apply only if every condition is true; otherwise do not count it as available funding.
- Grants: pursue only programs whose published scope actually matches this project's trading analytics. Do not spend the critical operating window on unrelated payment or blockchain grants.

## Truthful product status
The current MQL5 collector is a demo/fallback implementation. Shadow Mode does not place orders. Do not market generated demo signals as live broker data, verified trading performance, or investment advice. Reprice the offer only after a real data connector, provenance checks, and acceptance tests are demonstrated.

## Runway policy
- Keep a record of actual hosting costs and receipts.
- Do not add paid infrastructure until the money or credit is confirmed.
- If there is no paid customer or eligible credit, pause paid hosting before creating an uncontrolled bill; preserve the source and journal schema in Git.
